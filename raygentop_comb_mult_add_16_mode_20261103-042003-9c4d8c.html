The objective is area x delay (delay register to register, 2026-11-02): A times D, from VPR's total used logic block area (I/Os and empty grid tiles excluded), A, the delay D (the register-to-register figure since 2026-10-20) and the total routed wirelength, W (all three recorded under every objective). Each term is the median over the loop's seeds; the objective is formed from the medians. Beside every product stand the ratios against the baseline, A/A0 (area), D/D0 (critical path), W/W0 (wirelength) and their product, so the normalization can be read off. After each measured rewrite, the planner is given every measurement so far and asked for a revision. A revision is built and checked like the first rewrite, and the lowest product is kept, a later rewrite having to beat it by more than 4.60%. The loop stops when the planner declines to revise, when a revision does not lower the best product by more than 4.60%, when a revision fails its checks, or when 9 revisions have been made.
