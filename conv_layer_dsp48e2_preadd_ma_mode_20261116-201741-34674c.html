What the rewrite does not fold in

The accumulate adder (qadd, conv_layer.v:2859-2866, instantiated at 2810) is deliberately left in soft logic. Its two inputs are add_out_reg (the 32-bit accumulator feedback) and mul_out_reg, both 32-bit values from registers: the block's only additive input is PCIN, which may be driven only by another block's PCOUT or by 0, so the 32-bit feedback cannot enter the block, and neither operand fits the 27-bit A/D ports with B = 1. The adder also sits one register stage after the multiply (mul_out_reg), while the block is combinational and cannot supply that stage boundary. The eltwise mux (conv_layer.v:2807-2808) likewise stays outside.

Block count: 84 added (one per qmult instance), replacing the 84 baseline multiply tiles one for one; no cascade connections are used, so no two blocks need to be placed in the same column.

DESIGN_CHANGE: Each block uses only 16 of 26 usable pre-adder bits and 16 of 17 usable B bits, so a tile is less than a quarter used. If seq_mac instances that share one multiplier operand (the same b_flopped fanning out across a row of the systolic array, conv_layer.v:2453-2593) exposed their two multiplicands to one place, two products a0*b and a1*b could share a single block as ((a1<<16) + a0)*b with the two 32-bit halves split off the 48-bit P (the low product's carry into bit 32 would have to be corrected outside), halving the DSP tiles for the array. Also, if the accumulator register add_out_reg were narrowed to ≤ 26 bits and the elementwise-add mux moved ahead of the multiply stage, the accumulate could ride the PCIN/PCOUT cascade instead of a 32-bit soft adder.
