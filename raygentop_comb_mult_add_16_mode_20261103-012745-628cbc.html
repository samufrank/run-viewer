With U = u, V = v, W = wl = (8'hFF - u - v) mod 256 (raygentop.v:2862):

r = [U*Ru + V*Rv + W*Rw] mod 128

W's 8-bit wrap differs from the integer 255-U-V by a multiple of 256, and 256*Rw ≡ 0 (mod 128), so modulo 128 the term may be taken as (255-U-V)*Rw. Hence

r ≡ U*(Ru-Rw) + V*(Rv-Rw) + 255*Rw ≡ U*(Ru-Rw) + V*(Rv-Rw) - Rw (mod 128).

Each difference may be formed in 7 bits (a wrap by 128 changes the product by a multiple of 128), and 255*Rw ≡ -Rw (mod 128), which is the 7-bit negation 7'd0 - Xw.
