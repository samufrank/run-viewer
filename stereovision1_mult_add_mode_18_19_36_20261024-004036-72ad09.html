The objective is area x delay x wirelength (delay register to register, 2026-10-20): VPR's total used logic block area (I/Os and empty grid tiles excluded) times the critical path times the total routed wirelength. Each term is the median over the loop's seeds; the product is the product of the medians. Beside every product stand the ratios against the baseline, A/A0 (area), D/D0 (critical path), W/W0 (wirelength) and their product, so the normalization can be read off. After each measured rewrite, the planner is given every measurement so far and asked for a revision. A revision is built and checked like the first rewrite, and the lowest product is kept, a later rewrite having to beat it by more than 6.23%.
