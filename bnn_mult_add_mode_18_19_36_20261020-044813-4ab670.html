Beside every product stand the ratios against the baseline, A/A0 (area), D/D0 (critical path), W/W0 (wirelength) and their product, so the normalization can be read off. After each measured rewrite, the planner is given every measurement so far and asked for a revision. A revision is built and checked like the first rewrite, and the lowest product is kept, a later rewrite having to beat it by more than 5.11%. The loop stops when the planner declines to revise, when a revision does not lower the best product by more than 5.11%, when a revision fails its checks, or when 5 revisions have been made.
