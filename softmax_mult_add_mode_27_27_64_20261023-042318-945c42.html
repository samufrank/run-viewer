FPMult_PrepModule.$mul$softmax.v:2386$221(sum_in=FPMult_16) — left as it is. This is assign Mp = ({1'b1,a[9:0]} * {1'b1,b[9:0]}) at softmax.v:2386, an 11x11 unsigned mantissa product inside FPMult_PrepModule. There are 16 instances: FPMult_16 (softmax.v:2201) is reached once per expunit (softmax.v:1999 -> softmax.v:2180), and expunit is instantiated 8 times in mode3_exp (softmax.v:1073-1080) and 8 times in mode7_exp (softmax.v:1382-1389). The only reader of Mp is the pipe_1 field pipe_1[26:5] (softmax.v:2299), which is read only as MpC of FPMult_ExecuteModule (softmax.v:2262), so the product is not visible anywhere else and a rewrite would be legal. It is left for cost, not for correctness: see below.

Why it is left (cost)

  • Tiles. The baseline already has all 16 of these products on DSP tiles, two to a tile in two_mult_18x19 mode, 8 tiles of 132. One mult_add_int_27x27 block takes a DSP tile alone, and the 16 products share no operand (each expunit has its own a_reg and its own LUTout_reg2[31:16], softmax.v:1999), so no two of them can be packed into one 27x27 multiply by operand packing, and bx/chainin add addends, not products. A full conversion is therefore 16 blocks on 16 tiles, 8 tiles more than the baseline: +8 x 253779 = +2.03e6 area on a baseline of 4.15428e7 (+4.9%). A partial conversion is worse per product converted (8 converted = 8 tiles, 8 left = 4 tiles, 12 tiles total).
  • What soft logic would leave. Only the 22 flip-flops of the pipe_1 Mp field per instance, 352 flip-flops in all, would move into the blocks; there is no soft adder to absorb (the sum the id names is degenerate — FPMult_ExecuteModule does assign Mp = MpC at softmax.v:2441, so bx and chainin would both be tied to 0). Even assuming those flip-flops free whole clusters, that is 35.2 clusters = 9.8e5, and the conversion must add back per instance the reset-correction logic described below (2 flip-flops and one gate x 16, ~5 clusters, ~1.4e5). Net area is at best +1.19e6 (+2.9%), and realistically more.
  • Delay. The slowest stage of the design (9.44889 ns) and the next slowest (9.371 ns) both carry no multiply, so neither the removed stage (pipe_0 -> hard multiplier -> pipe_1) nor anything the rewrite touches is on them; the clock period stays 9.44889 ns. The rewrite's own stages stay well under it: register clock-to-Q 0.060 + wire register-to-block 1.52 + setup 0.019 = 1.60 ns in; 2.219 ns inside; out of the block 0.060 + wire into soft logic 0.96 + one correction gate + FPMult_ExecuteModule's 6-bit add and mux + wire to pipe_2 ~2.25, under 6 ns. The longest series of blocks in any stage would be 1 (no chaining is possible, each product is alone in its sum), so no stage gets slower either.
  • Wirelength. 8 more occupied DSP tiles spread the 16 expunit datapaths further apart; the 22-bit block-to-soft-logic nets replace 22-bit multiplier-to-flop nets of similar length, and pipe_0 must stay (its other fields feed pipe_1, softmax.v:2299), so no net wirelength is given back.
  • Objective. Area x delay x wirelength would rise by at least ~3% (>= 8.28e13 against the baseline 8.04768e13) with no compensating term. Nothing lowers the objective, so no rewrite is made.

Register stages, for the record (the conversion that was costed, not performed)

Before: operands a,b are registered into pipe_0 at edge k, the product is combinational in cycle k+1, and pipe_1[26:5] holds it after edge k+1 — 1 register stage between the operands at the module port and the product value, matching the block's 2-cycle operand-to-result latency if the block were fed from a,b directly (bypassing pipe_0, which would stay for its other fields). DESIGN_CHANGE: the 16 mantissa products are 11x11 and pairwise independent, so each one wastes most of a 27x27 block while costing a whole tile.
